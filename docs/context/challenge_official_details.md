# Amazon ML Challenge 2026 — Official Listing Details

**Host:** Amazon
**Category:** Hackathon
**Team size:** 2 – 4 members
**Registration deadline:** 22 Sep 2026, 11:59 PM IST
**Rewards:** Pre-Placement Interviews + prizes worth ₹2,25,000

---

## Overview

Amazon ML Challenge is a **two-stage competition** where students from engineering campuses across India get the opportunity to work on Amazon's dataset, bring fresh ideas, and build innovative solutions for a real-world problem statement. The top three winning teams receive cash prizes and certificates.

---

## Eligibility and Team Rules

- Open to all students pursuing **PhD / M.E. / M.Tech. / M.S. / MS by Research / B.E. / B.Tech.** full-time degrees across all engineering campuses in India
- **Graduation year:** 2027 or 2028
- Each team must have a **minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4** members
- Each team must have a **team leader**
- **Cross-college teams are allowed**
- One student **cannot** be a member of more than one team

### Registration (7 – 20 September)

Kickstart your journey by completing registration and setting up your team.

- Participants must provide a **valid AWS Builder Center Profile ID alias** — an invalid alias results in an **incomplete registration**
- Form a team of 2–4 members

### Best Practices Virtual Session (21 September)

Learn how to make the most of the tools available for the challenge. Topics covered:

- AWS Builder Center setup & benefits
- AWS Free Tier & compute credits
- AWS core services walkthrough
- Live demo: train, deploy & test an ML model

### ML Challenge — 72-Hour Hackathon (25 – 27 September)

- Teams receive the **problem statement and dataset on Day 1**, with until **Day 3** to build and submit solutions
- Each team must upload a **1–2 page document** outlining their approach, along with their **code / script / notebook in a zipped file**
- Teams can track performance through a **live leaderboard** reflecting rankings over the course of the challenge
- A **private leaderboard** will be revealed after the hackathon, based on the final solution submission and validation on the **complete test dataset**

### Machine Learning Round Results (2 October)

Results published for the **Top 50 teams**.

### Grand Finale (7 October)

Based on the leaderboard results and the approach document submitted at the end of Round 1, the **top 10 teams** are invited to present their solutions to **Amazon's Scientists**. Hosted live virtually on 7 October 2026.

---

## Competition Structure

### Stage 0 — Join the AWS Builder Center

Before registering, participants are **required** to create an AWS Builder Center Profile ID. A setup guide with step-by-step instructions is provided for creating the Profile ID and completing the required setup before registration.

### Stage 1 — Registration (7 – 20 September)

- Register for the challenge
- Provide a valid AWS Builder Center Profile ID alias (invalid alias → incomplete registration)
- Form a team of 2–4 members

### Stage 2 — 72-Hour Hackathon (25 – 27 September)

- Access to the problem statement and dataset on Day 1; build and submit until Day 3
- Live leaderboard tracking
- Upload a 1–2 page approach document plus a zipped file containing code, script, or notebook

### Stage 3 — Grand Finale (7 October)

Top 10 teams present to Amazon's Scientists, selected on leaderboard results plus the Round 1 approach document.
